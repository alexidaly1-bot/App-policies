MathMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

MathMate is a calculator app made by Alex Daly, a sole trader in Sydney, Australia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your sums and history

Everything you type, your history, your theme and your settings are saved only on your phone. We never see them and we don't have a server that receives them. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back up these settings to your own Google account.

No account

MathMate has no sign-up or login. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

Ads

The free version shows an ad now and then when you open the unit converter. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), basic device information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice anytime in MathMate under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Remove ads subscription

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active. You can cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Children

MathMate is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
