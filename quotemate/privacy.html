QuoteMate privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

  • Your records stay on your phone. We don't have servers that store them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The only outside service is for buying Plus: Google Play and RevenueCat handle the purchase.

Who we are

QuoteMate is made by Alex, ABN 71 373 747 255, Sydney, Australia ("we"). Contact: adu.customersupport@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type or add in the app: your business details, clients and their contact details, quotes, jobs, prices, deposits and payments, notes, photos and your logo. It's saved in the app's private storage on your device. We can't see it.

Because you enter details about your clients, you're responsible for handling their information fairly, as you would on paper.

Backups

  • Backup file: when you tap "Back up now", the app makes a file and opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes (Google Drive, email and so on) is your choice.
  • Android backup: if your phone's backup to Google is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. That's controlled by your phone settings and Google's privacy policy.

Buying Plus

Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card details. To check whether you have Plus, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives purchase information (such as the product, purchase date and an anonymous ID created for the app) and basic device details needed to process it. It doesn't receive your records.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders you set (jobs due, fittings, and 2 days before a free trial ends). Reminders are made on the phone.
  • Maps: the job map loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap. Like any website, their servers see your internet address and which map area is shown, not your client details. Finding an address on the map uses your phone's own map service (on most phones, Google's).
  • Ratings: the app may ask once if you'd like to rate it. That uses Google Play's own rating window. We don't see who rated or what.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to add a photo. Photos are copied into the app's own storage.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics tools.
  • The app never messages your clients. Call, text, email and share buttons open your phone's own apps.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything stored by the app. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us and we'll ask them to delete them.

Children

QuoteMate is for adults running a small business. It isn't aimed at children.

Your rights

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. Contact us to ask what we hold about you (usually nothing), to correct it, or to complain. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for big changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
